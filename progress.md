## The Plan

### Phase A — RECOMP (now → ~late July, weeks 1–7)
- Eat **at maintenance (~2,550 kcal)**, protein high, and **drive progressive overload** —
  adding reps/weight is the actual engine here, not the diet.
- Optional fine-tune: eat slightly **above** maintenance on training days, slightly **below**
  on rest days, netting ~maintenance for the week.
- **Success = lifts climbing + waist/photos improving while the scale stays ~flat.**
  Do NOT chase scale loss in this phase — flat is the goal.

### Phase B — MINI-CUT (~late July → ~mid-Sept, weeks 8–13)
- Drop to **~2,100–2,200 kcal** (~400 deficit). Target **0.4–0.6 kg/week** (~0.5–0.7% BW)
  → ~3–4 kg off an already-lean base is enough to reveal abs.
- Keep protein high (**160–170 g**) and **keep training heavy** to hold the muscle you built;
  only trim training volume if recovery tanks.
- Add **8–10k steps/day** to widen the deficit without having to cut food harder.
- This is when the **face leans out** — it's one of the last places to change, so be patient;
  manage water/sodium/sleep/alcohol for day-to-day facial bloat.

### Protein (the muscle-retention lever — both phases)
- **150–165 g/day** (≈1 g/lb, ~2.0–2.2 g/kg), across **3–4 meals** of ~35–50 g.
- Sources: eggs, chicken, lean pork, fish, tofu, edamame, Greek yogurt, milk, whey.

### Carbs & fat
- **Fat floor ~60–70 g** (hormones) — don't go under.
- **Carbs take the remainder** and fuel your lifts. Don't fear rice; keto isn't needed.

## Eating approach — Asian home cooking (the practical weekly lever)

- **Control the rice/noodle portion** — the #1 lever in home cooking. Use a fixed rice-cooker
  cup: **~1 cup cooked per meal on the cut**, a bit more on training days during recomp.
- **Anchor protein every meal** (1–2 palms): steamed fish, chicken, lean pork, tofu, eggs,
  edamame. If a home meal runs carb-heavy, **top up** with milk / Greek yogurt / a whey shake.
- **Pile on the veg dishes** (stir-fried greens, etc.) — volume keeps you full on fewer calories.
- **Watch the hidden calories:** cooking oil, oyster/sweet sauces, fried items, oily congee,
  and **bubble tea / sugary drinks** (easily 300–500 kcal each).

### Sample training day
- **Breakfast:** 3 eggs + congee/edamame _(or Greek yogurt + fruit)_
- **Lunch:** measured rice + steamed fish/chicken + greens
- **Pre-lift (1–3h):** banana + milk
- **Post-lift / dinner:** rice + lean protein (chicken thigh / lean pork / fish) + lots of veg
- **Night (if protein short):** Greek yogurt or a glass of milk

## Go-to dinners (budget · high-protein · quick)

Pair any main with **air-fried potato wedges** (skin on, ~1 tsp oil, season — don't salt; see
Custom goals). All ~$2–4/serving, 10–18 min.

| Tofu + egg stir-fry (+ frozen veg + low-sodium soy) | ~35 g | 12 min | $2.50 | 4:1 ratio + Asian-home friendly |

| Sheet-pan fish (frozen fillet + wedges on one tray) | ~35 g | 18 min | $4 | Omega-3 |

### How to cook the chicken thighs (boneless skinless)
Season first: pat dry, toss with **paprika + garlic powder + black pepper** (+ a little
low-sodium soy or herbs — go easy on salt for your 4:1).
- **Air fryer (alongside the wedges):** 400°F / 200°C, **18–22 min**, flip halfway.
- **Pan-sear:** medium-high heat, light oil, **~5–6 min per side**.
- **Oven sheet-pan:** 425°F / 220°C, **25–30 min**.
- **Done at 165°F / 74°C internal** (no pink, juices clear). Thighs are forgiving — hard to dry out.

## Nutrient must-haves (beyond calories & protein)

- **Fiber — ≥30 g/day (must-have).** Keeps you full on a cut, steadies energy, and cuts bloat so
  abs/face look leaner. Sources: beans/lentils, potato skins, oats, veg, fruit, berries.
- **Omega-3** — recovery + hormones + less inflammation. Fatty fish ~2×/wk, eggs, or a cheap
  fish-oil bottle.
- **Cut micros (easy to drop when eating less):** magnesium (greens, beans, nuts, dark
  chocolate — sleep/cramps), iron (red meat, beans, spinach), zinc (meat, eggs, seeds),
  calcium + vit D (dairy, sun), B12 (animal foods).
- **Hydration — ~3 L/day.** Drives your weigh-ins, gym performance, and facial bloat/skin.
- **Creatine — 5 g/day.** Cheapest, most proven supplement for *holding muscle + strength while
  cutting*. The water it adds goes to muscle, not your face.

## Custom goals

### Potassium : Sodium ratio — target **4:1** (committed)
**Why it fits:** higher K:Na lowers water retention → directly helps the **face-leaning** goal.

The math to actually hit 4:1: **sodium ~1,200–1,400 mg + potassium ~5,000 mg.** This is a
*sodium-restriction game first* — potassium has a practical food ceiling, so the ratio is won
by keeping sodium low (1 tbsp regular soy sauce ≈ ~950 mg Na — it's mostly about seasoning).

- **Daily targets:** potassium **~4,700–5,500 mg**, sodium **≤~1,300 mg**.
- **Playbook to hit 4:1:**
  1. **Control your own seasoning** — low-sodium soy sauce (~50% less), measure/limit added salt,
     season your own plate rather than the whole family pot.
  2. **Potassium base at every meal** — potato w/ skin (one large ≈ 900–1,600 mg — the cheat code),
     sweet potato, beans/lentils, spinach, banana, yogurt/milk, salmon, avocado, tomato, edamame,
     coconut water.
  3. **Dodge the sodium bombs** — instant noodles (~1,800 mg/pack), processed/cured meats, pickles,
     canned soup/broth, most takeout. On 4:1 days lean toward home/self-cooked over eating out.
- **Reality check:** potassium is rarely on labels, so the ratio is an *estimate* — judge the
  weekly trend, not a perfect daily number.

## How to track WITHOUT obsessing

- **Weigh daily, judge only the 7-day average.** Daily swings of 1–2 kg are water — pure noise.
  Recomp phase: expect ~flat. Cut phase: expect ~0.4–0.6 kg/week down. Same time, after
  bathroom, before eating.
- **Photos every 2 weeks** (same light, poses: front/side/back). The mirror lies; photos don't.
- **Waist weekly** at the navel.
- **Strength log** = your muscle-retention proxy. Lifts holding/climbing → muscle's safe.
- **Calibrate, don't count forever:** log food for ~1–2 weeks to learn your rice + protein
  portions, then eyeball it.
- **Don't:** weigh after a salty meal and panic, re-measure daily, or judge a 1-day jump.

**Green lights:** recomp = flat scale + rising lifts; cut = slow scale drop, lifts holding,
waist shrinking, photos improving.
**Red flags:** strength tanking, constant fatigue/cold, sleep/mood wrecked, or >1%/wk loss
for 2+ weeks → eat ~150–200 kcal more.
