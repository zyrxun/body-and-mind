# Body & Mind — Daily Food & Weigh-in Log

> Daily detail that feeds the weekly rollups in `progress.md`.
> Drop in what you ate (casually is fine) and your weigh-in — I'll append it, estimate
> kcal + protein + sodium/potassium per item, and total the day.

**Read this first:** estimates on home-cooked food are approximate (±10–20%; potassium even
rougher since it's rarely on labels). The point is the **trend** and the **week-level
relationship**, not any single day's number. Log daily, judge weekly, don't react to one day.

**Recomp-phase targets:** ~2,550 kcal · 150–165 g protein · potassium ~4,700–5,500 mg ·
sodium ≤~1,300 mg (K:Na target **4:1**)

**Daily habit to confirm:** ☕ 1 cup black coffee — if a day's log doesn't include it, I'll
remind you.
